Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who this covers

YAL Publisher is a single-user personal tool. The only person whose data it touches is its own operator. It has no visitors' data to collect: this website is a static page with no cookies, no analytics, and no forms.

2. What data the tool handles

  • TikTok OAuth tokens for the operator's own account, obtained with the operator's explicit consent. Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are never shared with any third party.
  • Basic profile info (display name) of the operator's own account, read once after login purely to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Video files created by the operator, transferred directly to TikTok's servers via the official API.

3. What is never collected

No data about any other person is collected, stored, or processed. Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. No tracking of any kind takes place.

4. Data deletion

Revoking the app's access in TikTok settings invalidates its tokens; local tokens can be deleted at any time by the operator. Contact oniongate007@gmail.com for any question.
