YAL Publisher

YAL Publisher is a personal, single-user publishing helper built and used by one independent creator.

It does one thing: after the creator finishes producing a short video on their own computer, the tool transfers that video file to the creator's own TikTok account as a draft, using TikTok's official Upload to TikTok feature (Content Posting API). The creator then opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, adds music and a caption, and publishes it manually.

What it is not

  • It is not a public service — there is no sign-up, and no one else can use it.
  • It never posts automatically. Every video is reviewed and published by hand.
  • It uploads at most one or two drafts per day.
